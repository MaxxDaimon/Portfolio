Typography

Archivo for everything structural (headings, body), JetBrains Mono for labels, tags, metadata and eyebrows. The mono adds the "technical" flavour that fits a game-tools designer.

Motion

Motion is subtle and purposeful — everything respects prefers-reduced-motion and falls back gracefully.

Scroll reveal

Sections fade + rise 32px as they enter view · 0.8s ease · 1.2s failsafe so content never stays hidden.

Ticker

Skills strip scrolls continuously under the hero · seamless loop.

Theme micro-interactions

Swatch spring on hover/press · animated active ring · accent ripple on apply · 0.45s global color ease.

Portrait carousel

Cross-fades between photos · slow 8s auto-advance · pauses on hover.

Hero rotation

Poster mode cross-fades; video mode hard-cuts · configurable interval.
